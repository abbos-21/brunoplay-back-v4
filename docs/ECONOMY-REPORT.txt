  spin Bronze    price    30  average win    21.1 stars  gives back 70%  (Heart or Frog: 0.00% of spins)
  spin Silver    price    75  average win    54.1 stars  gives back 72%  (Heart or Frog: 0.25% of spins)
  spin Gold      price   150  average win   112.5 stars  gives back 75%  (Heart or Frog: 1.00% of spins)
  spin Platinum  price   400  average win   312.0 stars  gives back 78%  (Heart or Frog: 3.91% of spins)
  spin Diamond   price  1000  average win   800.0 stars  gives back 80%  (Heart or Frog: 11.07% of spins)
  chest round price 20  average win 14.8 stars  gives back 74%

== What a player can earn for free
  a new player, 3 visits a day: about 4,780 coins a day ($0.0060)
  a committed player, 5 visits and 12 bonus ads: about 7,250 coins a day ($0.0091)
  first payout needs level 2, 0 days and 50,000 coins ($0.06); the most one account can ever take out is 99,000,000 coins ($123.75)
